Fixes for four layout bugs in 1.0.0.

- **Ask:** the chat history list stays inside the panel instead of running off its left edge.
- **New diagram:** diagrams and templates sit side by side again.
- **Board assistant:** answers read as normal paragraphs, and the Sends list folds away.
- **Diagrams:** a table or shape the assistant adds lands next to the one it connects to.
